      SAFETY & CONTEXT

      Know when a conversation should happen sooner.

      Do not wait for this website if you feel seriously unwell.

      Keep discussing at routine visits

      • Changes in energy, sleep or appetite
      • Questions about diet or supplements
      • New symptoms that are mild but persistent
      • Concerns about blood pressure readings
      • Questions about your pregnancy timeline

      Seek urgent medical advice

      • Severe or persistent headache
      • Blurred vision or seeing spots
      • Sudden swelling of face or hands
      • Severe upper abdominal pain
      • Difficulty breathing or feeling very unwell
